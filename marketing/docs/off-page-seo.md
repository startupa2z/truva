## Quality and measurement rule

Record a backlink only after its public URL resolves, visibly links to Truva, and is relevant to the business. Capture placement URL, target URL, first-seen date, relationship, and referral/lead evidence. Do not buy links, exchange links at scale, auto-post comments, or submit to irrelevant directories to reach ten. [Google's link spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam) treats those practices as manipulative. A useful link can be nofollow and still bring relevant buyers; measure that separately from ranking impact.
